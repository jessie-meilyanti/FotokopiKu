✨ About Me

Turning Real-World Problems
into Digital Solutions

Hi! I'm Jessie Meilyanti, an Informatics Engineering student with a deep passion for building technology that creates real, tangible impact in people's everyday lives.

I founded and built FotokopiKu, a full-featured e-commerce platform for photocopy and printing services — born from observing real pain points at a local copy shop. What started as a student project became a fully deployed platform serving customers across Jabodetabek.

Beyond code, I believe technology is most powerful when it's paired with empathy, leadership, and the courage to act on what you observe. That's the spirit I bring to everything I do.
